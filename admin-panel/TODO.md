
## Phase 4 — Website CMS

### 11. CMS overview and homepage builder
- Provide CMS overview metrics for published pages, active banners, homepage sections, draft content, scheduled campaigns, and recent content activity.
- Provide a visual homepage builder showing the default 14 storefront sections in order: Hero Banner, Shop by Equipment, Featured Products, Promotional Banner, Shop by Category, Best Sellers, Explore Snooker Cues, Snooker Balls & Accessories, Table Components & Lighting, Club & Professional Equipment, Bulk Enquiries, Shopping Benefits, Customer Reviews, and Newsletter.
- Support adding, editing, deleting, duplicating, enabling/disabling, drag-and-drop reordering, saving drafts, publishing changes, and responsive storefront preview modes.

### 12. Section configuration
- Support reusable section templates for Hero Banner, Product Carousel, Product Grid, Category Cards, Featured Products, Best Sellers, New Arrivals, Promotional Banner, Featured Collection, Testimonials, Shopping Benefits, Newsletter, and Bulk Enquiry.
- Section configuration supports heading, subtitle, product selection from the existing mock catalogue, layout, background style, and visibility controls.

### 13. Banner management
- Provide all banners, add banner, edit banner, deletion, desktop/tablet/mobile preview surfaces, headline/subtitle, CTA text/destination, placement, start/end scheduling, and active/inactive status.

### 14. Website pages and navigation
- Provide page management for About Us, Contact Us, Shipping Policy, Return Policy, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions with title, URL slug, rich-text-style content editing, SEO title/description, and publication status.
- Provide Header and Footer navigation management with category dropdowns, nested menu items, add/edit/delete-ready menu rows, visibility controls, and drag-and-drop ordering.
- All CMS functionality uses realistic mock data and frontend state, preserves the existing visual system, and does not require backend integrations.
